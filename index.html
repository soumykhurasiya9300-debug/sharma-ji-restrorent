Our Story

A kitchen built on family recipes.

House Of Sharma Ji began with a simple belief — that the best food is the food you'd serve your own family. Every masala is ground in-house, every gravy is slow-simmered, and every guest walks in as a stranger and leaves as a regular.

Tucked near Labour Chowk in Yadav Colony, our dining room is built for long conversations — kitty parties, birthday celebrations, get-togethers and quiet weekday dinners alike. Warm lights, comfortable seating, and a menu that spans North Indian classics to Indo-Chinese favourites.
